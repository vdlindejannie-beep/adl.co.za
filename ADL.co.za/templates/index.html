Maintenance

Preserving Your Leather's Life

Genuine leather requires proper nourishment to maintain its suppleness and natural shine. Regular conditioning prevents drying and cracking, ensuring your investment develops a rich, beautiful patina over time.

Check out our specialized Leather Care Cream & Cloth Kit available directly in the shop catalog to keep your items in pristine condition.

Quick Care Guidelines

  • ✅ Clean gently with a soft microfibre cloth.
  • ✅ Apply nourishing leather cream periodically.
  • ✅ Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and moisture.
  • ✅ Store in a breathable dust bag when not in use.
